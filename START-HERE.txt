START HERE
==========

This program applies to jobs for you (Naukri, and experimentally Instahyre, LinkedIn and Indeed).
You run it from a control panel that opens in your browser. Nothing personal is inside this folder:
you add your own resume, details and (if you want one) AI key in the control panel, and they are
saved only on your computer, in the "data" folder next to this file.

1. Unzip it to a short path, for example C:\agent-to-apply on Windows, or ~/agent-to-apply on a Mac.
   Not into a folder your computer syncs to the cloud, and do not move the folder after the first start.

2. Double-click ONE file:

       Windows :  Start.bat
       Mac     :  Start.command

   The first start installs everything (a few minutes, internet needed). Every start after that
   just opens the control panel. Keep the black window it opens in the background while you use it.

   Windows: if it says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   Mac:     once, before the first start, open Terminal and paste these three lines
            (only if you got this zip from someone you trust):

                cd ~/agent-to-apply
                xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine .
                chmod +x Start.command setup.sh

3. In the panel, follow steps 1 to 5 from the top. Rehearse (step 4) before you apply for real (step 5),
   and start with 1 to 3 applications.

Everything else is explained in README.md.
